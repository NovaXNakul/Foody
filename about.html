Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Usually 30 minutes, depending on your location.

Is there a delivery charge?

₹30 for orders below ₹500. Orders above ₹500 get free delivery.

How do I use a coupon?

Open the cart, type a code like FOOD10 and press Apply.

Which payment methods are supported?

UPI, Cash on Delivery and Credit/Debit Card.
